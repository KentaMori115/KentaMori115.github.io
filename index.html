About Me

I'm a backend and systems engineer with 8+ years of experience building high-throughput distributed systems in Go and Rust, including 5+ years on blockchain infrastructure across Ethereum, Cosmos SDK, and Solana.

My core specialty is performance engineering for compute-heavy workloads. I have 10+ years of focused work optimizing elliptic curve cryptography, from finite field arithmetic to massively parallel GPU (CUDA) computation. These are the same skills behind fast, cost-efficient AI training and inference.

I'm now applying that background to AI systems:

  • Model fine-tuning at scale. Fine-tuning experiments on Tongyi-MAI's 6B-parameter Z-Image base model across 1×, 4×, and 8× H100 SXM setups, comparing training parameters, throughput, and cost.
  • Production AI systems. Agents, retrieval, evaluation, observability, and cost control: the backend engineering that makes AI reliable in production.
  • Confidential and verifiable AI. Applying my experience with FHE, zk-SNARKs, MPC, and Intel SGX remote attestation to private and verifiable AI workloads.
